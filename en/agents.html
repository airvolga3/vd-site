The scheduled programme is sold through an agent network

Scheduled flight capacity is sold through regional General Sales Agents (GSA) and directly by the commercial department. The agent list on this page is demo content: the live network will come from department data.

Terms

What agent status gives you

— 01

Rates & priority

Agency rates on the scheduled programme and capacity priority on peak dates.

— 02

Block space

Guaranteed capacity on the flight for your regular volume — a contractual allotment.

— 03

Credit terms

Deferred payment and a credit limit under the agency agreement — no prepayment for every shipment.

— 04

Dedicated manager

A scheduled-programme manager: capacity by date, statuses, documents — one contact.
