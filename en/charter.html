Multimodal delivery

Door to door — when the flight is only part of the journey

Pickup, packing and securing, delivery to the departure airport, customs on both ends, the flight and the last mile — one contract, one responsibility, one manager. Road, rail and sea legs are added as needed.

OPTION A

Airport to airport

You bring the cargo to the aircraft — we handle the flight, loading and unloading. Faster and cheaper if your ground logistics is in place.

OPTION B

Door to door

We collect the cargo at the plant and deliver it to the consignee's site, customs and permits included. You sign one contract and receive one report.

Questions & answers

What people ask before requesting

What is the minimum cargo you accept?+

From one tonne. Small consignments fly on scheduled services, consolidated; a dedicated charter makes sense for cargo that exceeds the size or timeline of ordinary logistics.

How much does an An-124 charter cost?+

It depends on the route, cargo and timing — there is no flat price list in project logistics. Submit a request: we will give an indicative rate and aircraft availability within four business hours, free and with no obligation.

Do you carry dangerous goods?+

Yes, including most DGR classes. State the hazard class in your request — the manager will immediately tell you what permits are needed and what we take care of.

How soon can a flight depart?+

For urgent cargo it is a matter of hours: the charter duty desk works around the clock, and overflight permits on many routes are obtained within a day.

What do you need from us as the customer?+

At the start — only the cargo data: dimensions, weight, nature, origin and destination. The manager and the cargo planning centre handle the rest.
